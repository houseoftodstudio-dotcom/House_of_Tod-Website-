Who's Behind The Sound

The Founder

Karan Aherewal

Founder, House of Tod

Some people tell stories with words. Karan tells them with sound.

Over the last seven years, he's worked across radio, podcasts, films, theatre, branded content, original composition, and live performance—using music and sound as tools to shape emotion, build atmosphere, and create unforgettable experiences.

As a producer, composer, sound designer, and bassist, he thrives where creativity meets precision. Every project begins with the same question: What should this make people feel? The answer becomes the soundtrack.

Because long after people forget what they saw, they remember how it made them feel.
